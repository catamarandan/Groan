What GROAN does for Oceanus
🎯
Restoration Decision Scores. GROAN's DMAP-CAL engine recommends which site, which species, which technique — informed by all seven modules simultaneously, not just water temp and coral cover.

📈
Transplant survival correlation. GROAN connects outplant survival rates to the water quality, current regime, and thermal history at each specific site. Know why transplants fail — not just that they did.
⛵

Your ask from Oceanus

  • Validate DMAP-CAL recommendations against field reality

The cascade example

GMIN detects mangrove buffer loss → GROAN flags elevated sediment at nearby restoration sites → DMAP-CAL adjusts transplant species recommendation. Oceanus acts on causal intelligence.
